# Pan-India Urban Forestry, Parks & Tree Hazards — Statutory Jurisdiction Knowledge Base

## National Authority Architecture (All India)
Urban tree canopy preservation, botanical safety management, road median greenery, and emergency storm clearance across all States and Union Territories of India are overseen by:
1. **Municipal Tree Authorities & Horticulture Departments**: Statutory committees constituted under state tree protection legislation within every Urban Local Body (Municipal Corporation / Municipality).
2. **State Forest Departments (Social Forestry / Territorial Wings)**: Custodians of protected trees, urban reserve forests, institutional greens, and biodiversity corridors.
3. **Disaster Response Force (SDRF / City Disaster Response Units)**: Specialized emergency squads tasked with heavy motorized chainsaw clearance of uprooted trees during monsoons and severe convective storms.

## Scope of Grievances Handled
- Trees uprooted and fallen across major arterial roads, national highway stretches, railway tracks, or footpaths
- Large, cracked, or dried branches dangling precariously over roadways, live power conductors, or school entrances
- Overgrown tree foliage completely obscuring traffic signals, high-mast streetlights, or critical directional road signs
- Dead, diseased, hollow, or termite-infested roadside trees posing imminent structural collapse hazard onto vehicles or homes
- Tree roots violently breaking through municipal footpaths, underground water mains, or road asphalt surfaces
- Illegal felling, hacking, or unauthorized poisoning/girdling of heritage trees in urban public spaces

## All-India Statutory Acts, Legal Codes & Technical Standards
- **Constitution of India — Article 48A & 51A(g)**: Directive Principles and Fundamental Duties obligating the State and every citizen to protect and improve the natural environment, forests, and green canopy.
- **State Tree Preservation Legislation (Pan-India)**:
  - *Delhi*: Delhi Tree Preservation Act 1994, Section 8 (Restrictions on felling trees; mandatory permission and compensatory plantation of 10 trees per felled tree).
  - *Maharashtra*: Maharashtra (Urban Areas) Protection and Preservation of Trees Act 1975, Section 7 (Mandatory creation of municipal Tree Authority).
  - *Karnataka*: Karnataka Preservation of Trees Act 1976 (Section 8: Prior approval of Tree Officer mandatory).
  - *Telangana/AP*: Telangana Water, Land and Trees Act (WALTA) 2002, Section 28 (Tree protection, permission, and emergency felling).
  - *Tamil Nadu*: Tamil Nadu Forest Act & Municipal Tree Guidelines.
  - *West Bengal*: West Bengal Trees (Protection and Conservation in Non-Forest Areas) Act 2006.
- **Indian Forest Act 1927 / Wildlife Protection Act 1972**: Protection of notified forest species and nesting urban wildlife.
- **National Disaster Management Authority (NDMA) Urban Storm Protocols**: Pre-monsoon mandatory canopy pruning and rapid tree obstacle clearance SOPs.

## SLA Commitments & Escalation Matrix
- **Critical (Tree fallen across arterial roadway blocking traffic, snapped live electrical cable, or crushed vehicle)**: **2 Hours** (Emergency DRF / Horticulture flying squad dispatch).
- **High (Heavy cracked branch threatening pedestrians over school gate or busy sidewalk)**: **12 Hours**.
- **Medium (Overgrown branches blocking streetlights or traffic signboards)**: **48 Hours**.
- **Low (Routine colony pruning request, dry branch trimming)**: **120 Hours (5 Days)**.
- **Escalation**: Unresolved grievances escalate to Municipal Tree Officer -> Deputy Director (Horticulture) -> Director of Urban Biodiversity -> Municipal Commissioner.

## Authority Directory Routing
- Primary Authority: Municipal Tree Authority / Urban Biodiversity & Horticulture Directorate
- Emergency Response: City Disaster Response Force (DRF) / SDRF Fire & Emergency Services (112 / 101).
