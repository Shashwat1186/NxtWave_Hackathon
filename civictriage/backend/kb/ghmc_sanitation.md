# GHMC Sanitation & Solid Waste Management — Jurisdiction Knowledge Base

## Authority Overview
The Greater Hyderabad Municipal Corporation (GHMC) Directorate of Sanitation and Solid Waste Management
is mandated under the Telangana Municipalities Act 2019 and Solid Waste Management Rules 2016 to oversee
collection, segregation, transportation, and scientific disposal of municipal solid waste across Hyderabad.

## Jurisdiction & Coverage
- All public street waste bins, community dumpsters, and dumper placers
- Commercial and residential roadside garbage cleaning and sweeping
- Construction and demolition (C&D) waste dumping along public right-of-ways
- Public urination/defecation spots, bio-toilet maintenance
- Dead animal carcass disposal and biohazard cleanup
- Illegal garbage burning on roadsides, vacant plots, and stormwater drains

## Statutory Citations & Charter Standards
- Telangana Municipalities Act 2019, Section 154 (Duty to clean streets and collect waste)
- Solid Waste Management Rules 2016, Rule 15 (Duties and responsibilities of local authorities)
- GHMC Citizen Charter 2024: Mandatory clearance of overflowing community bins within 12 hours.

## SLA & Escalation
- Critical (biohazard waste, dead animal, burning garbage near schools): 4 hours
- High (overflowing dumpster blocking traffic/pavement): 12 hours
- Medium (routine sweeping complaint, uncollected door-to-door trash): 24 hours
- Escalation: Unresolved ward complaints escalate to Zonal Additional Commissioner (Sanitation).
