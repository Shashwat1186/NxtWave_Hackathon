# HMWSSB — Water Supply & Sewerage Board Knowledge Base

## Authority Overview
The Hyderabad Metropolitan Water Supply & Sewerage Board (HMWSSB) is responsible
for providing safe drinking water and sewerage services to the Hyderabad metropolitan
area. It handles all water main issues, pipeline leaks, and sewerage infrastructure.

## Jurisdiction
- Water supply pipelines and distribution networks
- Water mains (transmission lines and distribution mains)
- Overhead tanks and sump maintenance
- Sewerage networks and trunk sewers
- Sewage treatment plants
- Septic tank clearance on public land

## Issue Categories Handled
- Water main bursts and pipeline leaks
- Low water pressure or supply disruptions
- Sewage overflow onto roads or public spaces
- Blocked or collapsed sewer lines
- Contaminated water supply complaints
- Illegal water connections

## Escalation
- Emergency leaks (water main burst) have a 4-hour response SLA
- Non-emergency complaints escalate to divisional engineer after 24 hours
- Contamination complaints are immediately escalated to health authorities

## SLA Commitments
- Critical (water main burst, sewage overflow on road): 4 hours
- High (significant leak, supply outage): 24 hours
- Medium (low pressure, minor leak): 72 hours
- Low (billing dispute, minor query): 168 hours
