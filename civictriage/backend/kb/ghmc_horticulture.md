# GHMC Urban Biodiversity & Horticulture — Tree Hazard Knowledge Base

## Authority Overview
The GHMC Directorate of Urban Biodiversity & Horticulture has exclusive legal authority over
the preservation, maintenance, pruning, and emergency clearance of trees and green cover in
public rights-of-way, parks, and road medians across Hyderabad.

## Jurisdiction & Coverage
- Fallen trees obstructing traffic, vehicular movement, or footpaths
- Dangerous leaning branches threatening electrical lines, roofs, or pedestrians
- Overgrown foliage obstructing street visibility, signals, or streetlights
- Dead or termite-infested trees in public spaces requiring emergency felling

## Statutory Citations & Charter Standards
- Telangana Water, Land and Trees Act (WALTA) 2002, Section 28 (Tree protection and pruning guidelines)
- GHMC Citizen Charter 2024: Emergency road obstruction clearance by Disaster Response Force (DRF) within 2 hours.

## SLA & Escalation
- Critical (tree fallen across major road or live wire): 2 hours (DRF dispatch)
- High (heavy branch cracked hanging over pedestrian path): 12 hours
- Medium (routine pruning for streetlight illumination): 72 hours
- Escalation: Escalates to Director of Urban Biodiversity.
