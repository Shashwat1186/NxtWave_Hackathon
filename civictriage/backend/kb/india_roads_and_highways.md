# Pan-India Municipal Roads & Highways — Statutory Jurisdiction Knowledge Base

## National Authority Architecture (All India)
Road infrastructure across all States and Union Territories of India is partitioned into three distinct statutory tiers:
1. **Urban Local Bodies (ULBs / Municipal Corporations / Nagar Nigams / Municipalities)**: Responsible for all municipal roads, internal colony streets, sub-arterial corridors, footpaths, pedestrian subways, and municipal bridges under the 74th Constitutional Amendment (12th Schedule).
2. **National Highways Authority of India (NHAI) / MoRTH**: Statutory authority for all National Highways, Expressways, and bypasses under the National Highways Act 1956.
3. **State Public Works Departments (State PWD)**: Responsible for State Highways, Major District Roads (MDR), and Other District Roads (ODR).

## Scope of Grievances Handled
- Pothole repair, cratering, and asphalt resurfacing (small, medium, deep sinkholes)
- Road structural failure, pavement subsidence, rutting, and water-induced cavitation
- Damaged, broken, or missing pedestrian footpath slabs and curb stones
- Roadside stormwater drainage grates broken, missing, or blocked causing road flooding
- Unauthorized commercial and vendor encroachments on road right-of-ways and footpaths
- Damaged median barriers, missing crash barriers, and non-reflective signage on accident-prone stretches

## All-India Statutory Acts, Legal Codes & Technical Standards
- **Constitution of India — 74th Amendment Act (12th Schedule, Entry 4)**: Constitutional mandate placing urban roads, bridges, and footpaths under municipal bodies.
- **Motor Vehicles Act 1988 (as amended 2019), Section 198A**: Strict statutory liability imposed on designated municipal authorities, highway engineers, and contractors for failure to maintain road design and safety standards resulting in accidents or injury.
- **National Highways Act 1956 & Control of National Highways (Land and Traffic) Act 2002**: Exclusive authority over National Highway corridors and prohibition of unauthorized road cuts and encroachments.
- **Indian Roads Congress Standards (IRC:82 & IRC:SP:72)**: National technical code for pothole patching, bituminous surfacing, and road roughness indices.
- **State Municipal Corporation Acts (Pan-India)**:
  - *Telangana/Hyderabad*: GHMC Act 1955, Section 112 (Duty to maintain public streets).
  - *Maharashtra/Mumbai/Pune*: MMC Act 1888 Section 61(m) & BPMC Act 1949.
  - *Karnataka/Bengaluru*: Karnataka Municipal Corporations Act 1976 / BBMP Act 2020.
  - *Delhi*: Delhi Municipal Corporation Act 1957, Section 42 (Construction, maintenance of public streets).
  - *Tamil Nadu/Chennai*: Chennai City Municipal Corporation Act 1919 Section 203.
  - *West Bengal/Kolkata*: Kolkata Municipal Corporation Act 1980 Section 343.
  - *Uttar Pradesh/Lucknow*: UP Municipal Corporation Act 1959.

## SLA Commitments & Escalation Matrix
- **Critical (Deep pothole/sinkhole >15cm on National Highway, State Highway, or Major Arterial road near hospitals/schools)**: **4 Hours** (Emergency cold/hot asphalt patch).
- **High (Significant road crater, damaged culvert, broken drainage slab on bus route)**: **24 Hours**.
- **Medium (Colony road roughness, damaged footpath kerb, minor pothole)**: **72 Hours**.
- **Low (Aesthetic repainting, minor encroachment notice)**: **168 Hours (7 Days)**.
- **Escalation**: Unresolved Ward/Junior Engineer grievances escalate to Executive Engineer (Tier 1B), Zonal Municipal Commissioner, and the District Collectorate (Tier 2).

## Authority Directory Routing
- Primary Authority: Urban Local Body (ULB) / Municipal Corporation Road Maintenance Division / NHAI Project Implementation Unit (PIU)
- Contact: PWD/Municipal Engineering Cell across all Indian Municipal Wards.
