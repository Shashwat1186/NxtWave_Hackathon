# TSSPDCL & GHMC Electrical Wing — Streetlight & Grid Maintenance Knowledge Base

## Authority Overview
Public illumination across the Hyderabad metropolitan region is jointly managed by the
Telangana State Southern Power Distribution Company Limited (TSSPDCL) and the GHMC Electrical Wing.
TSSPDCL supplies 230V/415V low-tension distribution, while GHMC manages central monitoring system (CCMS)
LED street fixtures and high-mast towers.

## Jurisdiction & Coverage
- Roadway illumination on all municipal roads, arterial bypasses, and colony lanes
- High-mast lighting at major intersections, traffic roundabouts, and flyovers
- Damaged, leaning, or fallen electrical poles posing electrocution risk
- Exposed wiring, sparking junction boxes, and open feeder panels accessible to pedestrians
- Dark corridor remediation along designated women safety zones

## Statutory Citations & Charter Standards
- Electricity Act 2003, Section 53 (Provisions relating to safety and electric supply)
- GHMC Municipal Lighting Citizen Charter 2024: Mandatory repair of dark corridor within 24 hours.
- National Electrical Code (NEC) Section 7: Public safety grounding for road light poles.

## SLA & Escalation
- Critical (sparking live cable, hanging wires, pole collapse on road): 2 hours
- High (consecutive dark streetlights on arterial road or hospital route): 24 hours
- Medium (single non-functional LED luminaire in residential street): 48 hours
- Escalation: Escalates to TSSPDCL Divisional Engineer and GHMC Superintending Engineer (Electrical).
