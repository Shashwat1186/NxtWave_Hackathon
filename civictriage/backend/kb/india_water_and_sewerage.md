# Pan-India Water Supply & Public Sewerage — Statutory Jurisdiction Knowledge Base

## National Authority Architecture (All India)
Urban potable water supply, trunk transmission, and underground sewerage infrastructure across all Indian States and Union Territories are administered by:
1. **Dedicated Urban Water & Sewerage Undertakings**: e.g., Delhi Jal Board (DJB), Bangalore Water Supply and Sewerage Board (BWSSB), Chennai Metropolitan Water Supply and Sewerage Board (CMWSSB), Hyderabad Metropolitan Water Supply and Sewerage Board (HMWSSB), Kerala Water Authority (KWA), UP Jal Nigam.
2. **Municipal Corporation Water Works & Public Health Engineering Departments (PHED)**: In cities without separate statutory water boards, municipal engineering divisions operate water distribution and sewer networks under the 74th Constitutional Amendment (12th Schedule).

## Scope of Grievances Handled
- Major potable water pipeline bursts, high-pressure trunk main ruptures, and localized road flooding
- Low pressure, disrupted drinking water supply, or dry water taps in residential sectors
- Contaminated drinking water supply (turbid water, sewage infiltration into drinking water lines, foul odor)
- Blocked underground sewer mains, sewage backflow flooding roads, footpaths, or ground floors
- Open, broken, damaged, or stolen sewer and stormwater manhole covers on thoroughfares
- Overflowing domestic septic tanks, missing sewer connections, and illicit wastewater dumping

## All-India Statutory Acts, Legal Codes & Technical Standards
- **Constitution of India — Article 21**: Supreme Court of India precedent recognizing the fundamental right to clean and safe drinking water as an integral facet of the Right to Life (*Subhash Kumar v. State of Bihar* & *AP Pollution Control Board v. MV Nayudu*).
- **Water (Prevention and Control of Pollution) Act 1974 (Sections 24, 25 & 33A)**: Prohibits the discharge of untreated domestic sewage or industrial waste into any stream, lake, well, sewer, or on land. Empowered statutory boards to issue immediate closure and remediation orders.
- **Prohibition of Employment as Manual Scavengers and their Rehabilitation Act 2013**: Absolute prohibition on manual entry into hazardous sewers and septic tanks. Mandates Urban Local Bodies and Water Boards to deploy mechanized suction-cum-jetting machines and robotic rovers for sewer unclogging.
- **Bureau of Indian Standards (BIS IS 10500:2012)**: Mandatory national physical, chemical, and microbiological drinking water quality standards.
- **CPHEEO (Central Public Health & Environmental Engineering Organisation) Manuals**: Ministry of Housing and Urban Affairs (MoHUA) national guidelines for water treatment, pipe distribution networks, and sewerage design.
- **AMRUT 2.0 & Jal Jeevan Mission (Urban)**: National service delivery benchmarks requiring 135 liters per capita per day (lpcd) universal tap water and 100% sewerage network coverage.
- **State Water & Sewerage Acts**:
  - *Delhi*: Delhi Water Board Act 1998.
  - *Karnataka*: BWSSB Act 1964.
  - *Tamil Nadu*: CMWSSB Act 1978.
  - *Telangana*: HMWSSB Act 1989 Section 12 & Section 42.
  - *Maharashtra*: Maharashtra Municipalities & Water Supply Regulations.

## SLA Commitments & Escalation Matrix
- **Critical (Open/missing manhole cover, trunk water main burst flooding road, sewage entering drinking water network)**: **4 Hours** (Immediate barricading, emergency jetting, and valve throttling).
- **High (Overflowing residential sewer line, contaminated water supply to community)**: **12 Hours**.
- **Medium (Low water pressure, minor pipeline leakage, damaged valve chamber)**: **48 Hours**.
- **Low (Billing/meter complaints, new sewer line sanctioning)**: **168 Hours (7 Days)**.
- **Escalation**: Unresolved Ward/Section complaints escalate to Assistant Executive Engineer (AEE), General Manager (O&M), Municipal Commissioner, and District Magistrate (DDMA).
