# Pan-India Environmental Pollution & Industrial Hazards — Statutory Jurisdiction Knowledge Base

## National Authority Architecture (All India)
Environmental protection, industrial pollution monitoring, hazardous waste management, and ambient air and water quality enforcement across all States and Union Territories of India are overseen by:
1. **Central Pollution Control Board (CPCB)**: Apex national statutory organization under the Ministry of Environment, Forest and Climate Change (MoEFCC). Sets nationwide ambient standards, coordinates interstate pollution management, and operates the National Air Quality Index (NAQI) monitoring network.
2. **State Pollution Control Boards (SPCBs) / Pollution Control Committees (PCCs)**: Statutory regulatory bodies in each state (e.g. DPCC Delhi, MPCB Maharashtra, KSPCB Karnataka, TNPCB Tamil Nadu, WBPCB West Bengal, TSPCB Telangana, UPPCB Uttar Pradesh). Responsible for inspecting factories, granting/revoking Consent to Establish (CTE) & Consent to Operate (CTO), and monitoring industrial discharges.
3. **National Green Tribunal (NGT)**: Dedicated national judicial body established under the NGT Act 2010 with jurisdiction over environmental disputes and enforcement of civil compensation.

## Scope of Grievances Handled
- Illegal untreated industrial effluent discharge into storm drains, natural lakes, rivers, or agricultural fields
- Toxic industrial air emissions, black chimney smoke, unauthorized chemical furnace emissions, and burning of hazardous waste
- Severe illegal industrial noise pollution exceeding statutory decibel limits during night hours near residential areas and silence zones
- Illegal dumping, transport, or clandestine burial of hazardous chemical sludge, spent solvents, or e-waste
- Unlicensed polluting micro-enterprises (plastic shredding, battery recycling, electroplating) operating unlawfully within residential colonies

## All-India Statutory Acts, Legal Codes & Technical Standards
- **Constitution of India — Article 48A & 21**: Fundamental Right to a clean, pollution-free, and healthy environment (*M.C. Mehta v. Union of India* - Oleum Gas Leak & Taj Trapezium cases).
- **The Air (Prevention and Control of Pollution) Act 1981 (Union Legislation)**:
  - *Section 19*: Declaration of Air Pollution Control Areas across entire states.
  - *Section 21*: Mandatory requirement of previous consent before operating any industrial plant.
  - *Section 31A*: Power to issue binding directions including closure of unit or stoppage of electricity/water supply.
- **The Water (Prevention and Control of Pollution) Act 1974 (Union Legislation)**:
  - *Section 24*: Prohibition on use of stream or well for disposal of polluting matter.
  - *Section 33A*: Power to order immediate disconnection of power and water to defaulting factories.
- **The Environment (Protection) Act 1986 (Union Framework Legislation)**:
  - *Section 3*: Power of Central Government to take measures to protect and improve the environment.
  - *Section 15*: Criminal penalties (imprisonment up to 5 years and fines) for violation of environmental rules.
- **Hazardous and Other Wastes (Management and Transboundary Movement) Rules 2016**: Strict tracking of hazardous chemical waste manifests and mandatory disposal in authorized Treatment, Storage and Disposal Facilities (TSDF).
- **Noise Pollution (Regulation and Control) Rules 2000**: Decibel limits in industrial (75dB), commercial (65dB), residential (55dB), and silence zones (50dB).

## SLA Commitments & Escalation Matrix
- **Critical (Acute toxic chemical leak, direct discharge into municipal drinking water source, illegal chemical fire)**: **2 Hours** (Emergency inspection and police seizure).
- **High (Illegal industrial effluent pumping into stormwater nala, hazardous black smoke in residential zone)**: **12 Hours**.
- **Medium (Noise pollution from diesel generator sets, localized commercial dust emission)**: **48 Hours**.
- **Low (Stack emission compliance verification, consent certificate verification)**: **168 Hours (7 Days)**.
- **Escalation**: Unresolved complaints escalate to Regional Officer (SPCB) -> Member Secretary -> Chairman (SPCB) -> National Green Tribunal (NGT) Principal Bench.

## Authority Directory Routing
- Primary Authority: State Pollution Control Board (SPCB) / Central Pollution Control Board (CPCB) Regional Directorate
- Central Portal: CPCB SAMEER App & National Clean Air Programme (NCAP) grievance redressal interface.
