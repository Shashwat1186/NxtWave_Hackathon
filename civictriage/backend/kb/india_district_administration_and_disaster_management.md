# Pan-India District Administration & Disaster Management — Tier 2 Escalation Knowledge Base

## National Authority Architecture (All India)
Administrative coordination, multi-department grievance escalation, magisterial enforcement, public nuisance abatement, and disaster response across all 780+ districts of India are headed by:
1. **District Magistrate (DM) / Deputy Commissioner (DC) / District Collector**: The apex executive head of civil and revenue administration in every Indian district. Exercises supervisory jurisdiction over Municipal Corporations, Municipalities, Zilla Parishads, PWD, DISCOMs, Police, and Water Undertakings.
2. **District Disaster Management Authority (DDMA)**: Constituted in every district under the national Disaster Management Act 2005, chaired by the District Magistrate, responsible for emergency response, flood prevention, and crisis management.
3. **Sub-Divisional Magistrate (SDM) / Revenue Divisional Officer (RDO)**: Sub-district administrative authority holding magisterial powers for summary abatement of public nuisances.

## Scope of Grievances Handled
- **Tier 2 Administrative Escalation**: Unaddressed, delayed, or SLA-breached Tier 1 municipal grievances where line agencies (ULB, Jal Board, DISCOM, PWD) fail to act within statutory deadlines
- Inter-departmental coordination conflicts (e.g., Road cut dispute between Water Board and Municipal Corporation; cable laying disputes)
- Catastrophic urban flooding, monsoon waterlogging, flash floods, dam discharge alerts, and structural building/bridge collapse risks
- Massive encroachments on Government revenue land, national highways, railway margins, natural water bodies (lakes, tanks, cheruvus, wetlands, and nalas)
- Systemic environmental public health emergencies affecting multiple municipal wards or contiguous rural-urban fringes

## All-India Statutory Acts, Legal Codes & Technical Standards
- **Disaster Management Act 2005 (Union Legislation — Section 30 & Section 34)**:
  - *Section 30*: DDMA empowered to direct any government department, urban local body, or utility agency to take emergency preventive and remedial measures for public safety.
  - *Section 34*: District Magistrate empowered to commandeer resources, evacuate vulnerable populations, and order immediate demolition of hazardous structures.
  - *Section 51*: Penal provisions (imprisonment up to 2 years) for obstructing or failing to comply with directions of the Disaster Management Authority.
- **Code of Criminal Procedure 1973 (CrPC § 133) / Bharatiya Nagarik Suraksha Sanhita (BNSS) 2023 (§ 152)**:
  - Grants summary magisterial power to the District Magistrate or Sub-Divisional Magistrate to pass conditional orders for the immediate removal of any unlawful obstruction, danger, or public nuisance from any public roadway, river, or water channel.
  - Non-compliance constitutes a criminal offense under Section 188 of the Indian Penal Code (IPC) / Section 223 BNS.
- **Public Liability Insurance Act 1991**: Mandatory relief and compensation mechanisms for accidents arising from handling hazardous substances.
- **State Land Encroachment and Water Bodies Protection Legislation**:
  - Direct statutory duty on District Collectors to order eviction of unauthorized encroachers from floodplains, lake catchment zones, and drainage channels.

## SLA Commitments & Escalation Matrix
- **Critical (Urban flood emergency, breach of lake bund, structural building collapse, mass public hazard)**: **Immediate / 2 Hours** (Emergency DDMA deployment, NDRF / SDRF dispatch).
- **Escalated Tier 1 Breaches**: When a municipal ticket exceeds its SLA deadline (4h for Critical, 24h for High), the autonomous SLA system issues an administrative summons to the parent agency within **24 Hours**.
- **Public Default Branding**: Defaulting departments are flagged publicly on the District Dashboard for institutional accountability.

## Authority Directory Routing
- Primary Escalation Authority: Office of the District Magistrate & District Disaster Management Authority (DDMA)
- National Emergency Response Number: **112** (Universal Emergency Response Support System - ERSS across India).
