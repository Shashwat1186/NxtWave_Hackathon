# Hyderabad District Collectorate & Disaster Management — Tier 2 Escalation Knowledge Base

## Authority Overview
The Hyderabad District Collectorate serves as the apex revenue and disaster management authority for
Hyderabad District. It exercises supervisory oversight over municipal corporation bodies, water boards,
and state utility undertakings under the Disaster Management Act 2005 and Telangana Revenue Administration Codes.

## Jurisdiction & Coverage
- Tier 2 escalation for unaddressed or SLA-breached Tier 1 municipal grievances
- Inter-departmental disputes between GHMC, HMWSSB, and TSSPDCL
- Large-scale urban flooding, inundation, and seasonal monsoon emergency management
- Encroachment on government land, water bodies (lakes/tanks/nalas), and public properties
- Environmental pollution crises affecting multiple municipal wards

## Statutory Citations & Charter Standards
- Disaster Management Act 2005, Section 30 (Powers and functions of District Disaster Management Authority)
- Telangana Land Encroachment Act 1905, Section 6 (Eviction and summary forfeiture of unauthorized occupants)
- Code of Criminal Procedure 1973, Section 133 (Conditional order for removal of public nuisance)

## SLA & Escalation
- Critical (urban flood emergency, severe civic disruption): Immediate / 2 hours
- Escalated Breaches: Mandatory administrative summons issued to Tier 1 department within 24 hours
- Public Accountability: Publishing department default records on District Public Portal.
