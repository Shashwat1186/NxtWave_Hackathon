# GHMC Roads & Buildings — Jurisdiction Knowledge Base

## Authority Overview
The Greater Hyderabad Municipal Corporation (GHMC) Roads & Buildings Department
is responsible for the construction, maintenance, and repair of all roads,
footpaths, bridges, and flyovers within the GHMC limits in Hyderabad, Telangana.

## Jurisdiction
- All roads classified as municipal roads within GHMC limits
- Internal roads in residential layouts approved by GHMC
- Footpaths and pedestrian infrastructure
- Road drainage and stormwater drains along roads
- Road markings, signage, and barriers
- Bridges and culverts within GHMC limits

## Issue Categories Handled
- Pothole repairs (small, medium, large)
- Road surface damage (cracking, subsidence, rutting)
- Damaged footpaths and curb stones
- Road drainage blockages
- Encroachments on road margins
- Illegal structures on footpaths

## Escalation
- Ward-level complaints are handled at the ward office (Tier 1)
- Unresolved complaints after 24 hours escalate to the zonal office (Tier 2)
- State-highway-class roads are under NHAI or HMDA jurisdiction, not GHMC

## SLA Commitments
- Critical (major road damage, traffic hazard): 4 hours
- High (significant pothole, drainage issue): 24 hours
- Medium (footpath damage, minor road issue): 72 hours
- Low (cosmetic damage, minor encroachment notice): 168 hours
